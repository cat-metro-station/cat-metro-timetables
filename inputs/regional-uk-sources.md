# UK regional rail sources

The UK refresh job publishes scheduled passenger calls and service calendars. All cities use the same station and line IDs as the shipped map. The small matching table includes corridor stop sequences and identifiers, plus Belfast station names and coordinates for matching platforms. Belfast station coordinates come from Wikidata (CC0). [NaPTAN](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-public-transport-access-nodes-naptan) · [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing)

- **23 Great Britain passenger rail cities.** Contains Network Rail data © the year of the source extract. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The converter accepts the Network Rail CIF deployment from DirectConnectionFinder, uses CRS codes to match DfT NaPTAN stations, and retains the published dates, times, pickup and drop-off restrictions. It omits bank holidays where the source conversion does not contain the complete exceptions. [Network Rail Open Data](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/who-we-are/transparency-and-ethics/transparency/open-data-feeds/) · [CIF extract](https://jameswardvp.github.io/DirectConnectionFinder/data/timetable.json) · [Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · [UK bank holidays](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json)
- **Liverpool.** A few intermediate calls omitted by the CIF extract use running-time estimates from the Merseytravel Wirral and Northern line booklets. An independently obtained dated passenger timetable must corroborate every estimated call and its boarding rights. The verification GTFS and the booklets are not distributed. The timetable package links to the relevant operator booklets and marks individual estimates. [Merseytravel train timetables](https://www.merseytravel.gov.uk/timetables/train/) · [Passenger timetable used for verification](https://github.com/planarnetwork/gb-transit/releases)
- **Seven tram or metro cities.** Birmingham, Blackpool, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Manchester, Newcastle and Sheffield use the regional GTFS files from the Department for Transport's Bus Open Data Service, including light rail services in the Traveline National Dataset and NaPTAN station identifiers. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Service dates are derived from the selected operator's calendar and exceptions. [Bus Open Data Service](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/bus-open-data-service) · [Traveline National Dataset and licence](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/0447f8d9-8f1b-4a68-bbc8-246981d02256/traveline-national-dataset)
- **Belfast.** The National Transport Authority's GTFS for Northern Ireland supplies Northern Ireland Railways schedules. The converter matches station calls and retains boarding restrictions, stop times and service-date exceptions. Contains National Transport Authority data, adapted under CC BY 4.0. [Northern Ireland GTFS](https://www.transportforireland.ie/transitData/Data/GTFS_NI.zip) · [NTA dataset and licence](https://data.gov.ie/dataset/nta-gtfs) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Nottingham.** NET scheduled calls use the TravelWhiz East Midlands GTFS conversion of the Traveline National Dataset. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. GTFS conversion by TravelWhiz Ltd, CC BY 4.0. Individual journeys are matched to public NETTRAM operating profiles and UK government holiday dates. Unrecognised holiday rules or unmatched journey references stop publication for Nottingham. [TravelWhiz source notices](https://github.com/travelwhiz-ltd/GB-Bus-Train-Metro-GTFS/blob/main/DATA_SOURCES.md) · [NETTRAM service profiles](https://bustimes.org/services/tram-clifton-toton-nottingham-phoenix-park-hucknal/debug) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

For a fresh run use Python 3.9+ and curl, with a separate download directory. Networked runs retrieve upstream files again. To replay the exact retrieved inputs, add `--offline`. Repeat `--city` to limit a run to individual city IDs.

```sh
python3 tools/refresh_regional_uk.py --output packages --cache /tmp/cat-metro-uk
```
